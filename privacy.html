LocalClipper

Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

LocalClipper is a desktop tool that runs entirely on the user's own computer. Its author does not run any server for it and does not receive, collect, store, sell or share any user data.

Data handled on your computer

  • Videos, transcripts and clips: processed and stored only on your computer, in LocalClipper's output folder. Transcription and clip selection use AI models that run locally.
  • TikTok connection: when you connect TikTok, LocalClipper receives an access token, a refresh token, your TikTok account's open ID and display name. These are stored only on your computer, encrypted with Windows so only your Windows user can read them.

Data sent to third parties

  • TikTok: only when you click to send or post a clip, LocalClipper uploads that video (and, for direct posts, the caption and settings you chose) to your TikTok account through TikTok's official API. TikTok's Privacy Policy applies to anything sent to TikTok.
  • Video sites: if you paste a link, LocalClipper downloads that video from the site you linked.
  • Model downloads: on first use, LocalClipper downloads its speech-recognition model from Hugging Face. No user content is sent.

TikTok data we request

Scopes: user.info.basic (to show which account is connected), video.upload (to send clips to your drafts) and video.publish (to post clips you choose). LocalClipper does not read your videos, followers, messages or analytics.

Removing your data

Click "Disconnect" in LocalClipper to revoke the TikTok connection and delete the stored tokens. You can also remove access from your TikTok app settings. Deleting LocalClipper's folder removes everything it stored.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
